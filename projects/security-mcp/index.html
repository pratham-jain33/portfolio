// audit_site

Grade any website A+ to F

Point it at a domain. Get a plain-English report on its TLS, headers, and certificate.

  • Checks protocols, ciphers, HSTS, cert validity
  • Explains what's wrong and why it matters
  • Great for "is this checkout page safe?" questions

Is this CVE actually dangerous?

Cross-references CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list and EPSS scores.

  • "Exploited in the wild" vs "theoretical" — instantly
  • Plain-English severity, no CVSS decoder ring needed
